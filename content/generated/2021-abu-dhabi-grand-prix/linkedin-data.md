RaceIQ analysis: Abu Dhabi Grand Prix (2021)

Three separate, clearly-labeled metrics -- not one blended "who was best" score:

→ Average quick-lap pace: Max Verstappen led at 87.936s/lap.
→ Consistency (lap-time standard deviation across quick laps): Mick Schumacher was the steadiest.
→ Closing-pace heuristic (opening vs. closing quick-lap average): no eligible driver slowed down late this session -- Lando Norris had the strongest closing pace.

Method note: pace and consistency use every "quick lap" (filtered for pit stops, safety cars, and outliers) across the full session. The closing-pace figure is a heuristic comparing opening vs. closing quick-lap averages -- it is not a tire-degradation model.
