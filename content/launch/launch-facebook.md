I built something I've wanted for a while: a tool that looks past the finishing order of a Formula 1 race and shows you how it actually unfolded.

RaceIQ started as a personal Python project. It's now a real, working site that processes real race timing data -- pace, consistency, and closing-pace evidence -- for 20 historic F1 races, and explains the method behind every number.

Case in point, Abu Dhabi Grand Prix (2021): Max Verstappen led average pace, Mick Schumacher was the steadiest driver on track, and Lando Norris closed the race the strongest. All from real lap-by-lap data, not commentary.

Take a look, and if you want to know what a similar system could do for your own business's data, that's literally what I do for a living. https://raceiq.crouchdevelopment.com
